## Purpose
Top management reviews ISMS performance and decides on changes, resources and improvements.

## What the standard requires
Review at planned intervals. Inputs include status of previous actions, changes in external and internal issues, changes in needs and expectations of interested parties, performance feedback (nonconformities and corrective actions, monitoring results, audit results, objectives), feedback from interested parties, risk assessment results and treatment status, and improvement opportunities. Outputs include decisions on continual improvement and any need for ISMS changes, and resource needs. Retain results.

## Inputs (from earlier stages)
Scorecard, audit report, incident log, risk register, supplier and customer feedback, previous actions.

## Activities
1. Prepare a review pack covering every required input.
2. Hold the meeting with top management present and minuted.
3. Make decisions on risks, objectives, resources and improvements.
4. Record actions with owners and dates.
5. Communicate outcomes and track actions in the corrective action log.

## Deliverable
Management review pack, minutes and action log.

## Worked example: Orbitwise Cloud Ltd (fictional)
**Review 1 (month 10, Steering Committee plus CEO):**

| Required input | Summary presented |
|---|---|
| Previous actions | None (first review) |
| Context and party changes | New bank customer requires 24-hour breach notice |
| Performance | Objectives: OBJ-1 82%, OBJ-2 91%, OBJ-3 training 96% / click rate 11%, OBJ-4 test scheduled |
| Audit results | 2 major, 3 minor, 1 OFI (see stage 13) |
| Risk status | 5 High reduced to 1 High, 4 Medium |
| Improvement | Evidence automation |

**Decisions:** approve a tool for evidence automation; extend awareness budget; set restore test date; adopt 24-hour notice clause in the incident procedure. Actions logged as ACT-01 to ACT-04.

## Exit criteria
- [ ] Minutes prove decisions, resources and actions, not discussion only.
- [ ] Every required input is covered.
- [ ] Held before certification stage 2 and at least annually.

## Common audit findings and how to avoid them
- Review held but minutes lack decisions.
- Missing audit or risk inputs.
- Review never repeated.
