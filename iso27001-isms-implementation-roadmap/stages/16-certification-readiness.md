## Purpose
Confirm the ISMS is ready for the accredited certification body's two-stage audit and plan how to run it.

## What the standard requires
Certification is performed by an accredited certification body. Stage 1 reviews documentation and readiness; stage 2 assesses implementation and effectiveness. A certificate is generally valid for three years with annual surveillance audits and recertification at the end of the cycle. Exact timing and audit days are set by the certification body.

## Inputs (from earlier stages)
All previous stages, closed corrective actions, evidence register.

## Activities
1. Select an accredited certification body (check the accreditation scope and sector experience).
2. Run a final readiness check using the go/no-go checklist below.
3. Hold a mock stage 2 audit with sampling and interviews.
4. Brief staff and interview candidates; assign an audit coordinator.
5. Stage 1: provide scope, policy, SoA, risk assessment, audit and review records; fix gaps raised.
6. Stage 2: support evidence requests and interviews; agree nonconformities and corrective plans.
7. Receive the certificate, then plan surveillance audits and continual improvement.

## Deliverable
Readiness assessment, audit plan, audit coordination pack.

## Worked example: Orbitwise Cloud Ltd (fictional)
**Go / no-go checklist**

| # | Readiness criterion | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope, policy, risk methodology and SoA approved and current | Go |
| 2 | Risk assessment complete and treatment plan approved | Go |
| 3 | Controls operating with at least one full cycle of evidence | Go |
| 4 | Internal audit completed and findings graded | Go |
| 5 | Management review held with documented decisions | Go |
| 6 | No open major nonconformity; minors have approved plans | Go |
| 7 | Staff interviewed in mock audit can explain policy, objectives and role | Conditional (retrain 6 staff) |
| 8 | Certification body contracted and audit dates fixed | Go |

**Decision:** proceed to stage 1 once item 7 is closed. Mock audit outcome: 1 minor, 2 OFIs.

**After certification:** annual surveillance audits, internal audit and management review every cycle, recertification in year three.

## Exit criteria
- [ ] All checklist items are evidenced.
- [ ] Audit coordinator, schedule and room or remote access are arranged.
- [ ] Staff know what to expect; no one is coached to say anything but the truth.

## Common audit findings and how to avoid them
- Stage 1 passed with unresolved documentation gaps that become stage 2 majors.
- Internal audit and management review done after the certification audit was booked, with no time to correct.
- Evidence only exists for the last few weeks.
